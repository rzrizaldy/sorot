Privacy

Nothing leaves
this Mac.

No server

Your Mac does the listening with Apple's built-in speech models. No account. No upload. The one exception is lyrics, and only if you turn them on: then the song's title and artist go to lrclib.net.

Shut means off

The mic runs only while the card is open and you've pressed start. Fold it back into the notch and it's off.

Only you see it

It's set to stay out of screen shares and recordings. Nothing you say is saved.
