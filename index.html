The three things you can't forget

Mark a heading with ! and give it a phrase to listen for. Say it in your own words and it still gets ticked. About to run out of time before one? One click jumps you to it. At the end you see your time, your pace and what you missed.

Privacy

Nothing leaves
this Mac.

No server

Your Mac does the listening with Apple's built-in speech models. No account. No upload. It works on a plane.

Shut means off

The mic runs only while the card is open and you've pressed start. Fold it back into the notch and it's off.

Only you see it

It's set to stay out of screen shares and recordings. Nothing you say is saved.
