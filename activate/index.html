Your key looks like SOROT-eyJ2Ijox…. It works offline and has no expiry.

Right after you pay

  1. Stripe brings you to a Thank you page with your license key.

  2. Click Activate in Sorot. If your browser asks to open Sorot, allow it.

  3. Sorot says "Sorot Pro is on." The glasses in your menu bar now show Sorot Pro · your email.

Keep the key: copy it from that page, or save the page. You'll want it on a new Mac.

Pasting the key by hand

  1. Copy your key, the whole line starting with SOROT-.

  2. Click the glasses in your Mac's menu bar and choose Enter License Key… Or, in the notch, click I have a key on the Pro card.

  3. The key is already filled in from your clipboard. Click Activate.

Using a promo code

On the checkout page, click Add promotion code and type your code. If it makes the total $0, finish checkout anyway: you'll land on the same Thank you page with your key.

Something not working?

  • "That key doesn't look right." Copy it again, all of it, with no spaces before or after.
  • The Thank you page says "Almost there". Stripe hasn't confirmed the payment yet. Wait a moment and reload it.
  • Lost your key, or setting up a new Mac? Email support@sorot.app from the address you paid with, and we'll send it again.
